How a Street Food Kiosk Became The City's Favorite Hangout

In 2024, Coffee Hub was founded with a singular mission: to make premium cafe snacks and artisanal thick cold coffees accessible to every student and youth without burning a hole in their pocket.

While ordinary cafes charge ₹250+ for a simple cold coffee, we engineered a model where you get Thick Handcrafted Cold Coffee for ₹69, crispy Aloo Tikki Burgers for ₹49, and our viral sensation — Kulhar Pizza for just ₹99!

Our open-air cafe van features rustic mudha/wicker seating, lush trees, evening chill breezes, and a welcoming community of coffee lovers and foodies.

Why "Only Dine-In & Takeaway"?

We purposely choose not to list on delivery apps. When a Kulhad Pizza or crispy burger travels for 40 minutes in a delivery box, it loses its soul — the cheese hardens, the bun steams soggy, and the crispiness disappears. We want you to experience our food at its peak: sizzling hot and straight from the oven!
